## Family members
- **Climber sibling** - all-gripper limbs, no wheels; hybrid actuation: passive-latch
  hang (near-zero energy), ballistic brachiation (declutch to swing, re-engage as
  brake), central winch + tendons to haul the body up. Biomimetic principle: *spend
  energy only on transitions (grip/pull/release), coast the rest.*
