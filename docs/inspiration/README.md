# Inspiration — adopted design drawings and anatomical reference

## Adopted design drawings — DEC-36

- [Koala schematic.PNG](<Koala schematic.PNG>) — multiple views and segment table.
- [Side on.PNG](<Side on.PNG>) — side view with limb lengths and body dimensions.

Both were supplied by the maintainer on 2026-09-08 as drawings prepared with
the GPT app. DEC-36 adopts their general shape and nominal dimensions for V1,
including wheels at wrists and ankles. They are design references, not measured
anatomy or validated mechanisms. Their numerical conflicts and the controlling
dimension list are recorded in [`../body-layout.md`](../body-layout.md).
These two images and this provenance record are included in version control;
the unrelated third-party skeleton below remains local-only.
